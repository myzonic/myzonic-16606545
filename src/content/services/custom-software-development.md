# User Roles & Permissions

## Give the Right People the Right Access

Not every user should have access to every part of your software.

Custom permission systems can allow you to define what different users can view, create, edit or manage.

For example:

**Administrator**
Full system management and configuration.

**Manager**
Access to relevant business information, reports and team functionality.

**Employee**
Access to assigned tasks and relevant operational features.

**Customer**
Access to their own account, documents, orders or services.

Roles can be structured around your specific organisation.
