# Cover Letter Writing Services

## A Cover Letter That Adds Context to Your Resume

Your resume shows employers what you've done. Your cover letter explains **why you're interested in the opportunity and why your experience makes you a strong candidate.**

A generic cover letter can easily get overlooked. We create personalised cover letters that complement your resume and are written specifically around the role you're applying for.

Our cover letter writing service helps you communicate your:

* Career motivation
* Relevant experience
* Key achievements
* Transferable skills
* Professional strengths
* Interest in the company or position
* Understanding of the role
* Career goals

The result is a professional cover letter that adds substance to your application rather than simply repeating your resume.

# Tailored Cover Letters for Specific Roles

Different employers are looking for different qualities.

A cover letter written for a project management position should not read the same as one written for a marketing, healthcare, finance or technology role.

We tailor your cover letter around the position you're applying for, highlighting the experience and skills that are most relevant to that opportunity.

Where appropriate, we can incorporate information from the job description to ensure your application is focused and relevant.
